# Hortium – Privacy Policy

## English

### In short

TEOS IT does not collect, store or sell your personal data. Hortium has no user account, ads, analytics or tracking. The plant library and the list of towns are part of the app and work without internet.

### Data on your device

Your garden location, plantings, harvests, beds, tasks and settings (language, units, notifications) are stored only on your device. Home screen widgets read them on the same device. We have no access to this data.

Deleting the app permanently deletes all of it. A backup is a file you save yourself, for example to iCloud, Google Drive or Files; we never receive it.

### Location

Hortium uses your garden's location to time sowing and harvest for your climate and to show local weather. You can pick your town from the list built into the app, and that search never leaves your device, or allow access to your current location. Your current location is read only when you ask for it, never in the background.

If you use your current location, or your town is not in the list, the place is looked up by the system geocoder (Apple or Google). You can turn location access on or off in your device settings; the rest of the app works without it.

### Reminders

The daily summary and the watering, feeding, sowing and frost reminders are local notifications scheduled by the device itself. We do not send them through a server and do not collect any data about them.

### Purchases

Supporting development is voluntary and done through the App Store or Google Play. Payments are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your card details.

To verify purchases we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier and transaction details, but not your name or email address.

### Third-party services

The app connects to the internet only for the weather forecast, the system geocoder and purchases. For the forecast, the approximate coordinates of your garden (rounded to about 1 km) are sent to Open-Meteo ([open-meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com)); no other data from the app is sent with them. Purchases are handled by Apple, Google and RevenueCat. Their privacy policies apply to that data:

- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Open-Meteo Terms and Privacy](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms)
- [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/)

Town names come from GeoNames ([geonames.org](https://www.geonames.org), CC BY 4.0) and are built into the app.

### Children

Children can use Hortium safely, because the app does not collect personal data from anyone.

### Your rights

Since we do not store your personal data, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. For questions about data handled by Apple, Google, Open-Meteo or RevenueCat, contact us and we will help.

### Data deletion

Hortium has no user accounts. You can delete your data like this:

1. **Data on your device** (garden location, plantings, harvests, beds, tasks and settings): uninstall the app, or on Android open *Settings → Apps → Hortium → Storage → Clear data*. The data is deleted immediately and cannot be recovered. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you saved them until you delete them.
2. **Purchase data at RevenueCat** (anonymous identifier and transaction history): send an email to [contact@teosit.com](mailto:contact@teosit.com) with the subject "Delete my data" and the order number from your Google Play or App Store receipt (for example `GPA.1234-5678-9012-34567`). We will delete this data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Records of the payment itself are kept by Google or Apple according to their own policies and legal obligations; we cannot delete them.

### Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version in the app and on this page with the date of the last update.

### Contact

Send privacy questions to [contact@teosit.com](mailto:contact@teosit.com).

## Srpski

### Ukratko

TEOS IT ne prikuplja, ne čuva i ne prodaje vaše lične podatke. Hortium nema korisnički nalog, reklame, analitiku ni praćenje. Biblioteka biljaka i spisak mesta su deo aplikacije i rade bez interneta.

### Podaci na vašem uređaju

Lokacija bašte, sadnje, berbe, leje, zadaci i podešavanja (jezik, jedinice, obaveštenja) čuvaju se samo na vašem uređaju. Vidžeti na početnom ekranu ih čitaju na istom uređaju. Mi nemamo pristup tim podacima.

Brisanjem aplikacije svi ovi podaci se trajno brišu. Rezervna kopija je fajl koji sami sačuvate, na primer na iCloud, Google Drive ili u Fajlove; mi ga nikada ne dobijamo.

### Lokacija

Hortium koristi lokaciju vaše bašte da bi uskladio setvu i berbu sa vašom klimom i prikazao lokalnu prognozu. Mesto možete izabrati sa spiska ugrađenog u aplikaciju, a ta pretraga nikada ne napušta uređaj, ili dozvoliti pristup trenutnoj lokaciji. Trenutna lokacija se čita samo kada je zatražite, nikada u pozadini.

Ako koristite trenutnu lokaciju ili vašeg mesta nema na spisku, mesto određuje geokoder sistema (Apple ili Google). Pristup lokaciji možete uključiti ili isključiti u podešavanjima uređaja; ostatak aplikacije radi i bez njega.

### Podsetnici

Dnevni pregled i podsetnici za zalivanje, prihranu, setvu i mraz su lokalna obaveštenja koja zakazuje sam uređaj. Ne šaljemo ih preko servera i ne prikupljamo podatke o njima.

### Kupovine

Podrška razvoju je dobrovoljna i obavlja se preko App Store-a ili Google Play-a. Plaćanje obrađuju Apple ili Google, a mi ne vidimo podatke o vašoj kartici.

Za proveru kupovina koristimo uslugu RevenueCat, koja dobija anonimni identifikator i podatke o transakciji, ali ne i vaše ime ili email adresu.

### Usluge trećih strana

Aplikacija se povezuje na internet samo za vremensku prognozu, geokoder sistema i kupovine. Za prognozu se približne koordinate vaše bašte (zaokružene na oko 1 km) šalju usluzi Open-Meteo ([open-meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com)); uz njih se ne šalju drugi podaci iz aplikacije. Kupovine obrađuju Apple, Google i RevenueCat. Na te podatke primenjuju se njihove politike privatnosti:

- [Apple – politika privatnosti](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- [Google – politika privatnosti](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Open-Meteo – uslovi i privatnost](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms)
- [RevenueCat – politika privatnosti](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/)

Nazivi mesta potiču iz GeoNames-a ([geonames.org](https://www.geonames.org), CC BY 4.0) i ugrađeni su u aplikaciju.

### Deca

Hortium mogu bezbedno koristiti i deca, jer aplikacija ni od koga ne prikuplja lične podatke.

### Vaša prava

Pošto ne čuvamo vaše lične podatke, nemamo šta da vam pokažemo, ispravimo ili obrišemo. Za pitanja o podacima koje obrađuju Apple, Google, Open-Meteo ili RevenueCat obratite nam se i pomoći ćemo vam.

### Brisanje podataka

Hortium nema korisničke naloge. Svoje podatke možete obrisati ovako:

1. **Podaci na uređaju** (lokacija bašte, sadnje, berbe, leje, zadaci i podešavanja): obrišite aplikaciju ili na Androidu otvorite *Podešavanja → Aplikacije → Hortium → Memorija → Obriši podatke*. Podaci se brišu odmah i ne mogu se vratiti. Rezervne kopije koje ste sami sačuvali ostaju tamo gde ste ih sačuvali dok ih ne obrišete.
2. **Podaci o kupovinama u RevenueCat-u** (anonimni identifikator i istorija transakcija): pošaljite email na [contact@teosit.com](mailto:contact@teosit.com) sa naslovom „Brisanje podataka“ i brojem narudžbine sa računa iz Google Play-a ili App Store-a (na primer `GPA.1234-5678-9012-34567`). Podatke ćemo obrisati u roku od 30 dana i potvrditi vam emailom.

Podatke o samom plaćanju čuvaju Google ili Apple prema svojim pravilima i zakonskim obavezama; njih ne možemo obrisati.

### Izmene

Ako promenimo ovu politiku, objavićemo novu verziju u aplikaciji i na ovoj stranici i navesti datum poslednje izmene.
